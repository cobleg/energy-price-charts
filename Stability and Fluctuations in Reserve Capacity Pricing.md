No, the Reserve Capacity Price is **fixed for the entire Capacity Year** and does not fluctuate day-to-day based on the amount of capacity available on a given day.  
Here is how the pricing structure works under the Wholesale Electricity Market (WEM) Rules:

* **Annual Determination:** The Peak Reserve Capacity Price and the Flexible Reserve Capacity Price are calculated once per Reserve Capacity Cycle 1, 2\. The calculation uses a prescribed price curve based on the Benchmark Reserve Capacity Price (BRCP) and the total system-wide surplus of Capacity Credits assigned by AEMO relative to the Reserve Capacity Requirement for that year 1-4.  
* **Fixed Application Period:** Once determined, these prices apply for the entire Capacity Year, which runs from the start of the Trading Day on 1 October of Year 3 of the cycle to the end of the Trading Day on 1 October of the following year 5, 6\.  
* **Daily Settlement Rates:** For the purposes of daily financial settlement, the annual Reserve Capacity Price is simply converted into a daily rate. For example, the **Floating Daily Peak Reserve Capacity Price** is calculated by taking the annual Peak Reserve Capacity Price and dividing it by the exact number of Trading Days in that Capacity Year 7\.

**How daily availability affects payments:**While the *price* of a Capacity Credit remains fixed for the year, a provider's final *revenue* will vary based on their daily availability. If a facility fails to meet its Reserve Capacity Obligations (e.g., due to a Forced Outage), it is subject to **Capacity Cost Refunds** 8, 9\.  
Unlike the fixed capacity price, the penalty rate for these refunds **is dynamic and varies per Trading Interval** 10\. AEMO applies a "dynamic refund factor" based on the actual amount of spare capacity left in the system during that specific interval 11, 12\. Therefore, while the Reserve Capacity Price does not change daily, failing to be available on a day when the system is short on capacity will result in much higher financial penalties than failing to be available on a day with plenty of spare capacity 11, 12\.
