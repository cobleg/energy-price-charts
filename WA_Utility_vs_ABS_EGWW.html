ABS 5204.0 EGWW national GFCF — asset-type composition

WA utility data: Bottom-up project-level annual asset investment programs for the six WA state-owned utilities. Headline figures are the "Total Asset Investment Program" or equivalent parent row per utility per FY; child projects are surfaced in the drill-down panel.
ABS 5204.0 EGWW: Table 64 — Gross Fixed Capital Formation by Industry by Type of Asset, Electricity, Gas, Water and Waste Services industry, Australia-wide, chain volume measures. Annual, FY ending 30 Jun. Includes public corporations and private businesses; this is the right benchmark for state-owned WA utilities.
ABS 5625.0 EGWW: Table 8 — Trend Chain Volume Measures, EGWW sheet, quarterly summed to FY. Excludes public-sector business units, which is why this aggregate is much smaller than 5204.0 — Synergy, Western Power, Horizon Power, Water Corporation, Bunbury Water and Busselton Water are all public corporations and so are excluded from 5625.0 but included in 5204.0.
Caveat: WA utility data covers FY2021–FY2027 with actuals through FY2024 and forward forecasts thereafter; ABS 5204.0 data is available to FY2025; ABS 5625.0 quarterly is available to Dec 2025 (incomplete FY2026). Cumulative comparison is FY2021–FY2025 to keep the windows aligned.
