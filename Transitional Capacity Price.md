  
The Transitional Capacity Price is a specific pricing arrangement within the Wholesale Electricity Market (WEM) designed to provide financial stability and manage the transition for existing electricity generation assets.  
Here's what the Transitional Capacity Price implies:

* **Purpose and Intent**  
  * **Revenue Certainty:** It aims to provide revenue certainty for existing generation facilities.  
  * **Risk Amelioration:** The mechanism is intended to ameliorate perceived revenue risks associated with new, "sharper" capacity pricing and procurement frameworks.  
  * **Orderly Transition:** It helps existing assets (often called "Transitional Facilities") navigate the market reforms without undue financial disruption, thereby facilitating the smooth entry of new capacity resources.  
  * **Debt Servicing:** The intent is to set the price at a level that enables existing generation providers to service debt associated with capacity investments required to meet the reserve capacity target.  
* **Applicability (Who it applies to)**  
  * The Transitional Capacity Price applies to "Transitional Facilities".  
  * A Transitional Facility is defined as an existing facility (excluding Demand Side Programmes) that was assigned Capacity Credits for the 2018 Reserve Capacity Cycle. This can include both entire facilities and their separately certified components.  
* **Duration**  
  * This transitional arrangement is time-limited, typically for 10 **years** from the implementation of the new arrangements.  
* **Pricing Mechanism (How it works)**  
  * **Price Band:** Instead of receiving the fluctuating, market-determined Reserve Capacity Price, Transitional Facilities operate within a specific price band with a floor (minimum price) and a cap (maximum price).  
  * **Values:**  
    * For the 2019 Reserve Capacity Cycle and subsequent Transitional Reserve Capacity Cycles, the initial floor (Trans\_Floor) was **$114,000 per megawatt (MW)**, and the initial cap (Trans\_Ceiling) was **$140,000 per MW**.  
    * These values are adjusted annually for **Consumer Price Index (CPI) movements**.  
    * Specific figures for later years, like the 2025-26 Capacity Year, indicate a floor of $155,418.93/MW for Transitional Facilities.  
  * **Calculation:** The Facility Monthly Reserve Capacity Price for a Transitional Facility (or a component thereof) is calculated using a formula that ensures the price remains within the defined floor and ceiling: `Min(max(Reserve_Capacity_Price, Trans_Floor), Trans_Ceiling) / 12`. The result is then divided by 12 to provide a monthly rate.  
  * **Dual Capacity Products:** With the introduction of Peak Capacity and Flexible Capacity products, there are distinct transitional price calculations for each. For example, the Component Monthly Flexible Reserve Capacity Price for a Transitional Component has a specific formula that incorporates both Flexible and Peak Reserve Capacity Prices, with a floor but no explicit cap.  
  * **Contrast with New Entrants:** New capacity resources typically receive a capacity price under a different formula, although they may have the option of a five-year fixed-price lock-in. Demand Side Management resources are generally priced on the main formula but are ineligible for the five-year price lock-in.

In essence, the Transitional Capacity Price provides a protected, predictable revenue stream for a defined period for older, existing generation assets to ease their integration into the reformed market, preventing sudden financial shocks while new market price signals become more established for new investments
