About This Dataset

Pipeline Overview

This dashboard presents data produced by a three-stage automated pipeline:

  • export_sent_out_trading_prices_join.py — Executes a normalised SQL join on Google Cloud SQL to align sent-out generation volumes with reference trading prices at each 30-minute interval.
  • run_sent_out_trading_prices_join.ps1 — PowerShell automation wrapper for the extraction script.
  • create_monthly_summary_statistics.py — Aggregates the joined extract into monthly statistics across 14 metrics.

The two source tables had incompatible timestamp formats (ISO vs local AWST wall-clock) that required normalisation before joining. No UTC conversion is applied — all timestamps represent local Australia/Perth (AWST) time.

Generation Metric Definitions

  • Gen Sum: Total sent-out energy (MWh) for the month — useful for trend and capacity analysis.
  • Gen Mean / Median: Typical output per 30-min interval; median is robust to outliers.
  • Gen Min / Max: Operating range; identifies unusual low/high output periods.
  • Gen Std: Output variability — higher values indicate less stable generation.
  • Intervals: Expected ~1,440–1,488 per month (48 intervals × days). Values outside this range indicate partial months or data anomalies.

Price Metric Definitions

  • Reference Trading Price: The time-weighted average of Market Clearing Prices for each Dispatch Interval within a Trading Interval, per WEM Rules clause 7.11A.1(b).
  • Price Mean / Median: Typical market price; median better reflects baseline conditions excluding price spikes.
  • Price Min / Max: Full price range experienced in the month, including negative prices and administered cap events.
  • Price Std: Market volatility — higher values indicate more price volatility within the month.
  • Note: The WEM is transitioning to 5-minute settlement, which will replace the 30-minute Reference Trading Price mechanism.
